Zhenjie Zhao is currently a postdoc researcher at Nankai University. He received the Ph.D. from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). Prior to HKUST, he received the B.Eng. and M.Eng. from Nankai University. He was also a visiting scholar at University of Maryland, College Park. His research interest is human-centered AI to develop AI systems that are more beneficial and user-friendly for humans. Current topics are:
  • Embodied AI (grasping and manipulation, point cloud, language agent, etc.)
  • AI Evaluation
We welcome undergraduate/graduate students and visiting interns/scholars. Please drop me an email with your CV if your are interested in these topics or have any question.
