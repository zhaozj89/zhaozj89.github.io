Zhenjie Zhao is currently an associate professor at Nankai University. He received the Ph.D. from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). Prior to HKUST, he received the B.Eng. and M.Eng. from Nankai University. He was also a visiting scholar at University of Maryland, College Park. His research interest is human-centered AI to develop AI systems that are more beneficial and user-friendly for humans. Current topics are:
  • Embodied AI (grasping and manipulation, point cloud, language agent, etc.)
  • AI Evaluation
